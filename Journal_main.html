Every place
leaves something.

This is not a guide to the best things to do. It is a shared journal of the places that stayed with us long after we left them.

About this journal

A few words on where this came from.

This started as a place to store travel notes before we forgot them. It became something more like a record of how different places felt rather than what they looked like in photos.

The entries here are honest. Some trips were quiet and slow. Some were busier than expected. All of them left something behind.

We are not professional travelers or photographers. We are just a group who moved around, paid attention, and wrote what each place felt like.

Journal entries

Dahab, Sinai

We went expecting a simple beach town. We did not expect the calm, the color of the water, or how easy it was to lose track of time there.

Dahab felt slow in the best possible way. Mornings started with the sea completely clear and still, and by sunset the whole town seemed to soften even more. There was something about the mix of desert, water, and low-key streets that made everything feel lighter.

What stayed with us most was how unforced it all felt. Nothing in Dahab tried too hard. The beauty was already there in the coastline, the mountains in the distance, and the quiet rhythm of the place itself.

Santorini, Greece

The photos do not lie exactly, but they leave out the feeling of walking through it in person.

During the day, everything was bright in a way that felt honest. The white buildings did not just look clean, they reflected the sun so strongly it softened everything around them. The blue-domed rooftops stood out, not loud, just certain. Like they belonged exactly where they were, against the sky and the sea.

The island was not just something to look at, it was something to move through, slowly, without needing to frame it.

Dubai, UAE

We expected it to feel loud all the time. It actually felt calmer at night than we thought it would.

During the day, Dubai can feel sharp and a little too controlled. At night it changed completely. The lights reflected everywhere, the buildings felt less harsh, and the empty spaces between them stood out more.

We did not leave thinking of landmarks first. We left thinking of reflections, roads, and how different the whole city felt after sunset.

Ain Sokhna, Egypt

This trip ended up being one of the most memorable.

We remember summers spent in Ain Sokhna with family and relatives, where simple moments turned into lasting memories. Those gatherings were filled with warmth, laughter, and a strong sense of connection that brought everyone closer. The quiet surroundings and shared experiences created a unique atmosphere that we continue to admire.

Even now, thinking back on those times evokes a deep sense of nostalgia and appreciation for the bonds we built during those special days.

Riyadh, Saudi Arabia

We never expected that going to Saudi Arabia would feel so life changing, especially when visiting the capital. It grew into a second home, like a place that was hard to let go of.

Riyadh is one of the very few places that would hug you and welcome you with open arms, in her embrace, it always felt like a place you don't need to figure out who you are in order to be welcomed. It welcomes you regardless of who you are, where you came from, what your ethnicity is and where you're even headed. Riyadh is the place to go when you feel lost, when you don't know where you're headed, it is the place for the perfect atmosphere, for the perfect horizons, clear skies, clear air and it will feel absolutely majestic with every movement you decide to take.
