Accommodations

Where I've stayed during my global travels.

From city hotels to quiet retreats ,these were stops along the way that I won’t forget.

Santorini Stay

Golden East Hotel

Staying at the Golden East Hotel felt calm and refreshing, with easy access to the beach and a relaxed seaside atmosphere that made it a comfortable place to unwind after a day of exploring. It’s the kind of stay where mornings feel slow and peaceful, and evenings are best spent just enjoying the quiet coastal vibe.

I’d recommend it especially if you’re looking for a laid-back beach trip and don’t need anything too luxurious or fast-paced. It’s a great fit for couples, solo travelers, or anyone wanting a simple, relaxing base by the sea. If you’re after a very lively hotel experience or luxury resort-style amenities, you might prefer something more upscale—but for a peaceful, easy stay, it works really well.

My overall rating: ★★★★☆

Ain Sokhna Stay

Rozina Beach Resort

Rozina Beach Resort is a quiet, clean, and family-friendly escape where life feels slower and more peaceful, giving you the space to truly unwind. From the moment you arrive, the calm atmosphere and well-kept surroundings make you feel comfortable and at ease, while the safe, welcoming environment makes it perfect for families

Whether you’re enjoying the fresh sea breeze, relaxing by the pool, or simply spending quality time with your loved ones, every moment here feels simple, warm, and genuinely relaxing.

My overall rating: ★★★☆☆

Dahab Stay

Ceasar Camp

Caesar Camp offers a completely different kind of experience from the usual resorts and hotels—it’s all about simplicity, connection, and living close to nature. Staying in a camp here feels more personal and authentic, where the focus shifts from luxury to atmosphere, from busy schedules to slow, meaningful moments.

The setting is relaxed and welcoming, with basic but comfortable services that cover everything you need, while still keeping that raw, natural charm. Whether you’re sitting by the sea, sharing meals under the open sky, or enjoying the quiet rhythm of Dahab, the experience feels genuine, peaceful, and refreshingly different.

My overall rating: ★★★★☆

Dubai Stay

Pullman Dubai Downtown Hotel

Pullman Dubai Downtown offers a beautifully clean and well-organized stay where every detail feels carefully taken care of, creating a sense of comfort from the moment you arrive. The rooms and common areas are consistently spotless and modern, reflecting a high standard of cleanliness and attention to detail

With a wide range of services—from room service and dining options to a pool, gym, and concierge support, you have everything you need in one place, making your stay smooth, easy, and truly enjoyable

My overall rating: ★★★★★

Saudi Arabia Stay

Novotel Riyadh Al Anoud

Novotel Riyadh Al Anoud offers a completely different kind of experience from the typical bustling high-rises—it’s all about urban calm, streamlined comfort, and finding your own rhythm within the city’s relentless energy. Staying here feels more like a quiet reset, where the focus shifts from the rush of the streets to the serenity of a perfectly curated, modern space.

The setting is polished and efficient, with a clean aesthetic that strips away the noise of the outside world, leaving you with everything you need and nothing you don't. Whether you’re watching the city lights drift across the skyline from your window, enjoying the quiet stillness of the upper floors, or simply taking a moment to breathe before your next meeting or exploration, the experience feels anchored, intentional, and refreshingly balanced.

My overall rating: ★★★☆☆
