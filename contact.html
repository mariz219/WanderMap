Quick answers

Things people usually want to know.

Are the journal entries all personal trips? +
Yes, every entry in the journal is a place one of us has personally been to. The writing is based on our own experiences, not sourced from other guides or reviews.
Do you take requests for destinations to cover? +
Sometimes. If a place is suggested enough, and one of us ends up going, it will make its way into the journal. The best way to suggest one is through the contact form above.
Can I use your photos or writing? +
Please reach out first. Personal, non-commercial use with proper credit is usually fine. Commercial use or republishing without permission is not.
How often do you add new journal entries? +
Whenever there is a new trip worth writing about. There is no fixed schedule — quality over frequency.
